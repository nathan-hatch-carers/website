About Hatch

Care for the Carers who Care for the Kids.

Hatch is the independent, carer-led community platform for NSW foster and kinship carers — led by carers, built on technology, and independent of every government department and service provider in the sector.

Mission & vision

What we're working toward.

Mission

Care for the Carers who Care for the Kids.

Vision

A foster care system where every carer feels valued, supported and heard, so placement stability becomes the norm rather than the exception, and every child in care has the chance to grow up in a stable, nurturing home.

Our story

Why Hatch exists.

Carer wellbeing is deeply connected to child wellbeing. Carers who feel connected and supported are better placed to provide consistent, high-quality care — and the system on its own can't always give them that.

Hatch was founded by a 20-year veteran of the NSW out-of-home-care sector and the founder of Waddle, a technology company, alongside carers with decades of lived experience. Four years of platform development later, Hatch is run by its board and its community, and it grows month on month.

Leadership

The people behind Hatch Carers.

A board driving month-on-month growth, carer leaders with decades of combined lived experience, and founders pairing deep sector knowledge with technology expertise.

Marcie McGowan

CEO, Founder, Director

Marcie has dedicated her career to making a difference to the lives of Australia's most disadvantaged children. She's a passionate social justice advocate who isn't afraid to ask the hard questions or get stuck into messy problems. Drawing on 20 years experience working in foster care, Marcie is no stranger to navigating complex systemic dynamics, and believes positive and productive working relationships are key to addressing the sector's most pressing needs.

"Co-founding and leading Hatch Carers has been my most challenging and rewarding professional experience to date. Working amid the quagmire of systemic problems requires a level of perseverance that humbles me daily. Thankfully, it's not a solo effort—leading this initiative is made possible by the dedication and expertise of the team around me."

Marcie holds qualifications in Psychology and Social Impact. She has worked for both large and small OOHC NGOs in various casework and management roles during her 15-year tenure in the NSW OOHC sector.

Nathan Andrews

CTO, Founder, Director

My past 25 years have been quite a journey, filled with challenges, excitement and diverse experiences. I've transitioned from managing and performing music in Sydney to spending 15 years in software engineering and leadership. This path led me to build Waddle, which was sold as a best-in-class Fintech company to Xero.

At the Reserve Bank of Australia, I gained early exposure to software and economics while tackling the challenge of modernising bulky and outdated banking systems. At Tyro, I experienced top-tier engineering practices that support rapid changes in a secure environment. Co-founding Waddle allowed me to learn every aspect of running a business, including software that was eventually adopted by major banks globally. On any one given day I might have attended a marketing strategy meeting; lead a UX designer interview; facilitated an architectural retrospective with the senior engineering team, and hopefully ended the day writing some code myself. I consider myself fortunate to have engaged with nearly every aspect of running a company, learned countless lessons, and witnessed many experiments come to fruition.

Ken Raison

Director, Volunteer

My wife and I have been carers for a long time (23 years) with a full provision of care types and I have provided personal advocacy for carers across the state for 20 years. That advocacy experience wears many caps — sometimes you are the knowledge expert, sometimes you are the counsellor, the consultant, the writer and the voice of the carer so that both the carer and their children in care are afforded the opportunities they are entitled to have.

This experience together with my many tertiary qualifications places me in a unique position to assist other carers and hence why I've joined Hatch Carers — an equally unique group of experienced carers and professionals with similar ethos.

Kids and their carers are my Raison d'être.
