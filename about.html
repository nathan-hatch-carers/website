About Hatch

Care for the Carers who Care for the Kids.

Hatch is the independent, carer-led community platform for NSW foster and kinship carers — led by carers, built on technology, and independently governed, separate from every government department and service provider in the sector. Partners can support Hatch; none of them control it.

Mission & vision

What we're working toward.

Mission

Care for the Carers who Care for the Kids.

Vision

A foster care system where every carer feels valued, supported and heard, so placement stability becomes the norm rather than the exception, and every child in care has the chance to grow up in a stable, nurturing home.

Our story

Why Hatch exists.

Carer wellbeing is deeply connected to child wellbeing. Carers who feel connected and supported are better placed to provide consistent, high-quality care — and the system on its own can't always give them that.

Hatch was founded by a 20-year veteran of the NSW out-of-home-care sector and the founder of Waddle, a technology company, alongside carers with decades of lived experience. Four years of platform development later, Hatch is run by its board and its community, and it grows month on month.

Leadership

The people behind Hatch Carers.

A board driving month-on-month growth, carer leaders with decades of combined lived experience, and founders pairing deep sector knowledge with technology expertise.

Marcie McGowan

CEO, Founder, Director

Marcie has dedicated her career to making a difference to the lives of Australia's most disadvantaged children. She's a passionate social justice advocate who isn't afraid to ask the hard questions or get stuck into messy problems. Drawing on 20 years experience working in foster care, Marcie is no stranger to navigating complex systemic dynamics, and believes positive and productive working relationships are key to addressing the sector's most pressing needs.

"Co-founding and leading Hatch Carers has been my most challenging and rewarding professional experience to date. Working amid the quagmire of systemic problems requires a level of perseverance that humbles me daily. Thankfully, it's not a solo effort—leading this initiative is made possible by the dedication and expertise of the team around me."

Marcie holds qualifications in Psychology and Social Impact. She has worked for both large and small OOHC NGOs in various casework and management roles during her 15-year tenure in the NSW OOHC sector.

Nathan Andrews

CTO, Founder, Director

Nathan's past 25 years have taken him from managing and performing music in Sydney to 15 years in software engineering and leadership — a path that led him to build Waddle, sold as a best-in-class fintech company to Xero.

At the Reserve Bank of Australia, he got early exposure to software and economics while modernising legacy banking systems. At Tyro, he saw top-tier engineering practice firsthand. Co-founding Waddle let him learn nearly every part of running a company — marketing strategy, product hiring, architecture reviews, and still writing code himself — with software that was eventually adopted by major banks globally.

Ken Raison

Director, Volunteer

Ken and his wife have been carers for 23 years, across a full range of care types, and Ken has provided personal advocacy for carers across NSW for 20 years — as knowledge expert, counsellor, consultant, writer and voice for carers, so that both carers and the children in their care get the opportunities they're entitled to.

That experience, together with his tertiary qualifications, is what drew him to Hatch Carers — an equally unique group of experienced carers and professionals with a similar ethos.

"Kids and their carers are my Raison d'être."
