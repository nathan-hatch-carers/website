Insights

Turning carer experience into insight.

Carers talk to each other on Hatch in ways they can't always talk to government, agencies or service providers. Over time, that trust can help Hatch see what's happening for carers, and share what we learn constructively.

How it works

Community first. Insight follows.

Hatch exists for carers first, not to collect data about them. A trusted community means honest conversation, and honest conversation helps carers support each other. Over time, recurring experiences, in aggregated and anonymised form, can give us and the wider sector a clearer picture of what helps carers keep caring.

State of Foster Care

An emerging body of work.

Carer wellbeing is closely tied to whether care stays available. As the Hatch community grows, we're developing a framework for understanding the conditions that help carers keep caring, and the early signals that show when those conditions are under strain.

Wellbeing

Whether a carer has the day-to-day capacity to sustain the role.

Agency

Whether a carer feels able to raise a hand or ask a question.

Support

Whether help is actually reachable when it's needed.

Trust

Whether a carer believes the system will respond well to what they share.
