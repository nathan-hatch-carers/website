Insights

Turning carer experience into insight.

Carers will tell each other things on Hatch they will not tell government, agencies, or service providers. That's the asset — and the reason Hatch can surface what's actually happening for carers, earlier than official channels can.

How it works

Community first. Insight follows.

Hatch exists for carers first, not to collect data about them. But a trusted community naturally produces something valuable: an honest, first-hand picture of what helps carers keep caring, and what makes the role harder than it needs to be.

State of Foster Care

An emerging body of work.

Care availability is a function of carer wellbeing. As the Hatch community grows, we're building a framework for understanding the conditions that keep carers caring — and the early signals that show when those conditions are breaking down.

Wellbeing

Whether a carer has the day-to-day capacity to sustain the role.

Agency

Whether a carer feels able to raise a hand or ask a question.

Support

Whether help is actually reachable when it's needed.

Trust

Whether a carer believes the system will respond well to what they share.

This work is still developing alongside the community it draws on. As findings are ready to share responsibly, they'll be published here.
