Inside Hatch

What you get here.

Four things carers tell us matter most — talked about peer to peer, not case to case.

Connect

Meet other carers who understand what the role is actually like, day to day.

Support

Ask questions, share the hard moments, and get support from people who've navigated it before you.

Knowledge

Learn from the lived experience of carers who've faced the situation you're facing now.

Voice

Add your experience to a stronger, more accurate picture of what carers actually need.

Why Hatch is different

A space that belongs to carers.

Hatch is independent of the organisations responsible for care arrangements. That matters because carers need somewhere they can talk openly, with people who understand the role — without it touching a placement or a file.

Independence

Not government funded. Not service-provider embedded. A space that's fully carers' own.

Shaped by carers

Built and shaped by people with real lived experience of fostering and kinship care.

Peer-led

Designed around carer-to-carer connection, not case management.

Complementary

Works alongside government and service providers — not instead of them.

What this makes possible

When carers connect, something bigger happens.

Every conversation adds to a clearer picture of what helps carers keep caring — and what makes the role harder. Hatch exists for carers first. Insight is something that grows out of a trusted community, not the reason carers are invited into it.

System impact

Turning carer experience into insight.

A trusted community produces honest conversation. Honest conversation produces collective knowledge. That knowledge can help government, NGOs and the wider out-of-home-care system understand what supports — and what undermines — sustainable caring.
