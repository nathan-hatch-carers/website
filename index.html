Why now

The system is being rebuilt in real time.

Four signals mark the most consequential moment for carer-focused organisations in two decades.

13,500
NSW children currently in out-of-home care
$1.2B
2025–26 NSW child protection package — the largest single investment in the system's history
~1,000
Carers lost in NSW each year, three years running
20%
First carer allowance increase in 20 years, effective January 2026

Source: NSW Government 2025–26 Budget; Talbot and Dean system review.

The insight everything is built on
Carers will tell each other things on Hatch they will not tell government, agencies, or service providers.
— Hatch's foundational insight

Official channels carry formal responsibilities that make full candour difficult, however well they're run — carers naturally hold back in any setting connected to placement decisions. Hatch is the space that's fully the carers' own: a structural condition no other organisation in the sector currently meets.

What we are

The only independent, carer-led community of its kind.

Hatch is independent, carer-led, and technology-enabled. Independent enough to surface the carer reality safely. Connected enough to help the system learn.

Independence

Not government funded. Not service provider embedded. A complement to both, trusted because we belong to carers.

Lived expertise

A community of professionals doing skilled work, holding knowledge no caseworker can. We treat that expertise as legitimate and valuable.

The upstream lever

Carer wellbeing is the upstream lever in a multi-billion-dollar system. When carers stay, placements stabilise.

Team credibility

A board driving month-on-month growth, six carer leaders with 80+ years of combined caring experience, and founders pairing 20 years in the NSW system with 20 years building technology businesses.
