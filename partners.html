Partners

Help strengthen the community around carers.

Hatch welcomes partnership with government, philanthropy and the corporate sector — including funding, contracts and collaboration where it makes sense. What partnership doesn't include is control.

How independence works

Working with Hatch, without controlling it.

Hatch is independently governed. Partners can fund, contract with, or collaborate with Hatch — but none of that gives a partner control over the community, editorial say over carer voice, or identifiable access to carer conversations. That arm's-length structure is what lets Hatch work constructively with the system, while remaining trusted by the carers inside it.

Government & sector

Understand carer experience

The NSW out-of-home-care system is being rebuilt in real time, and the carer voice has been named as a missing input. Hatch is independent enough to surface the carer reality safely, and constructive enough to be a partner in the rebuild rather than a critic outside it.

We work with government and sector bodies on understanding what's actually happening for carers, and on the conditions that support sustainable care — alongside existing services and peak bodies, never in their place.

Philanthropy

Build independent infrastructure

Carer wellbeing is the upstream lever in a multi-billion-dollar system. When carers stay, placements stabilise. Philanthropic support helps fund the platform, the community team, and the research that keeps Hatch independently governed — able to work with government and the sector without ever handing them control of the community.

Corporate partners

Support practical initiatives

Corporate partners help fund practical resources and research that improve the everyday experience of NSW foster and kinship carers — support that aligns with values like family wellbeing and community resilience, delivered through a genuine, structured partnership rather than a one-off sponsorship.
