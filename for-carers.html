Independence & safety

Can I actually talk here?

Yes. Hatch is independently governed. Government and sector organisations may support or partner with Hatch, but none of them control the community, what carers can say, or who gets access to it. That's what lets you talk with people who understand the role without it touching a placement or a file.

What you contribute inside the community stays private. Hatch doesn't pass identifiable conversations back to your agency, caseworker, government or any partner. Where the community's collective experience helps us understand what carers need — or helps inform the sector — it's shared only as anonymised patterns, never as individual conversations.

Questions

A few things carers ask.

Is Hatch really independent, even if it partners with government?

Yes. Hatch can partner with government and sector organisations, but it remains independently governed. No partner gets to control the community, decide what carers can talk about, or access identifiable conversations. That's the structural condition for trust.

Who can join?

Authorised NSW foster, kinship, grandparent and therapeutic carers. Hatch holds all carer types under one roof, and works alongside ACCOs, AbSec and SNAICC — never in their place.

Will anything I say get back to my agency or caseworker?

No. What you contribute inside the Hatch community stays private. Hatch doesn't pass identifiable conversations back to your agency, caseworker, government or any partner. Where the collective experience of carers helps inform our research or feedback to the sector, it's anonymised.

Is there an app?

A native app is in development. In the meantime, the community runs on our existing platform.
