Independence & safety

Can I actually talk here?

Hatch is independent of the organisations responsible for your care arrangements — not government funded, not service-provider embedded. That's what lets you talk with people who understand the role without it touching a placement or a file.

It also means Hatch is complementary, not adversarial: we work alongside government and service providers, not instead of them. The community exists for carers first. Anything Hatch learns from that community is a by-product of trust, never the reason carers are invited in.

Questions

A few things carers ask.

Is Hatch really independent?

Yes. Hatch holds no funding or contracts from any government department or service provider that would make carers hold back. That's the structural condition for trust.

Who can join?

Authorised NSW foster, kinship, grandparent and therapeutic carers. Hatch holds all carer types under one roof, and works alongside ACCOs, AbSec and SNAICC — never in their place.

Will anything I say get back to my agency or caseworker?

Hatch is a space that's fully your own, independent of the organisations responsible for your care arrangements. That independence is the point.

Is there an app?

A native app is in development. In the meantime, the community runs on our existing platform.
